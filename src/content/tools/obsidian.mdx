## How I use it

Obsidian is my thinking space: daily briefings, project progress logs, templates, and long drafts. The vault is synced with my server, so the same markdown files are editable and version-controlled from every machine.

## Where it loses

The moment other people need to see or edit something, I move it to [Notion](/tools/notion/). The two are complements, not competitors.

Full comparison: [Obsidian vs Notion: Choosing the Right Tool for Each Use Case](/blog/notion-or-obsidian/).
