## Why I used it

OpenCode was capable but heavy (~318 MB per agent) and shipped a full cockpit I did not need. Pi fixed both problems: it is small, starts fast, and only loads the extensions I install.

## Why it is retired

Nothing broke. Jcode simply went further on the same axis I cared about: cheaper sessions, faster startup, and a stronger harness. See my [Jcode review](/tools/jcode/) for why I switched.
