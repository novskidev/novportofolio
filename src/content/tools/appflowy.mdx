## Why it is on the list

AppFlowy is one of three Notion replacements I recommend. Its Personal plan is US$0, and because it can be self-hosted, the data lives on your own machine or server instead of someone else's cloud.

See also: [AFFiNE](/tools/affine/) and [Anytype](/tools/anytype/).
