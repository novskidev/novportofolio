## Why it is on the list

Excalidraw replaces Miro for most of what I actually draw: system diagrams, flows, and rough brainstorming. Open the site, draw, and share a link when someone else needs to join. If you need Miro-level templates and big workshop boards, Miro still wins. For everyday sketches, Excalidraw is faster and free.
