## Why it is on the list

Cartesia is my free replacement for ElevenLabs. Open the [playground](https://play.cartesia.ai/), pick a voice, paste text, and you get natural speech in seconds. The Free plan gives 20K credits a month, which is about 27 minutes of audio at 1 credit per character.

## Where it loses

The Free plan is for personal use only. If you need a commercial license or instant voice cloning, the Pro plan costs US$5 a month. See [Cartesia pricing](https://cartesia.ai/pricing).
